# Your folder

Put your resume or CV here: a PDF, Word (.docx), text or Markdown file. Add more files if you
like (an older CV, a list of projects, course certificates as text). Then tell your AI app
**"Set me up"**. It reads these files, asks only what they do not say, and writes your answers
to `about-me.md`.

- `about-me.example.md` shows the questions. Your AI app copies it to `about-me.md` for you, or
  you can copy it and fill it in yourself.
- `profile.md` is written by your AI app when the dashboard is not installed: every fact it
  found, with the file it came from, and the questions still open. Check it once.

Everything you put here stays on your computer: Git never uploads this folder (only this guide
and the example are shared).
