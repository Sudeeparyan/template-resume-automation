# Applicant-tracking (ATS) rules

- One column. Standard headings (Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education,
  Certifications). Body text only: nothing important in headers, footers, text boxes or tables.
- No images, icons, charts, skill bars or photos. Ordinary round bullets.
- Selectable text in reading order. A PDF made from a scan or an image fails.
- Use the posting's exact wording for a skill the person really has ("Power BI", not only "BI
  tools"), in a real sentence or the skills list. Never hide keywords, repeat them to game a
  filter, or add a skill they do not have.
- Dates in one format throughout. Full URLs rather than linked words, so they survive parsing.
- PDF unless the application asks for Word; keep the DOCX so the person can edit it.
- Common fonts (Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, Garamond, Times) at 10 to 12 pt body text, margins of
  at least 1.5 cm.
