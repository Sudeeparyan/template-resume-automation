# Interview prep

Read `AGENTS.md` first. Find the job and the resume that was actually sent (App mode: the job's
application folder under the profile's `data/output/`; AI-only: its folder in `my-jobs/`). Every
line of that resume is fair game for the interviewer.

In App mode, first read `career ws summary --profile <id>` and
`career ws profile --profile <id>` to use the current job status and candidate evidence.

Deliver, briefly:

1. The ten most likely questions for this role and company, with why each is likely.
2. For each, which of their real experiences answers it best (name the role or project and the
   evidence line), a short opening line in their own voice, and the point to land.
3. Risky areas: requirements they do not meet, gaps, anything on the resume they must be able to
   defend in detail. For each, an honest way to answer ("I have not used X in production; I used
   Y, which is close because …").
4. Technical topics to revise, at the depth their evidence supports.
5. Four good questions to ask the interviewer.
6. Practical details from the invitation: time, format, who, what to bring.

Keep team work as team work and their own part as theirs. Do not invent dates, numbers,
ownership, publications or results. Never give immigration advice; for permission-to-work
questions, use only what their profile states. Offer a mock interview: ask one question at a
time and give short feedback on each answer.

Change the job's status to interview only when they confirm the interview (see
`track-applications`).
