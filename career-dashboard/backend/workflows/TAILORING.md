# TAILORING

Read the selected profile and evidence registry. Verify the exact posting, market, requirements, location and work authorization. Research public company facts and keep the hiring-manager stage candidate-blind. Select evidence and projects under this profile's contract, then prepare a draft. Compile, inspect each page and validate the PDF, source claims and evidence map. Job fit, requirement coverage and artifact QA are separate.
